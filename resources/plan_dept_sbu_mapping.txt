This is a mapping table. It maps a plan ID to respective department number and SBU name for all plans. A plan maps to one department. Each department also maps to only one SBU. There are a total of 6 SBUs - Apparel, Food, Consumables, Hardlines, Home and ETS. Food and Consumable are also referred to together as FC while Harldines, Home and ETS are together called General Merchandize (GM) too.
